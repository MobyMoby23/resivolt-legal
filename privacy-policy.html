ResiVolt — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

ResiVolt ("the app") is a field toolkit for residential electricians. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: the app keeps your data on your own device and does not collect, transmit, or sell it.

Information the app stores

Everything you enter — your business details, customer names and contact information, quotes, saved prices, and app settings — is stored only on your device, using your device's local storage. It is not sent to us or to any server we control, and we have no ability to access it.

Information we collect

None. The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking, or accounts. We do not operate a server that receives your data, and we do not build a profile of you.

When information leaves your device

Your information only leaves your device when you choose to send or move it:

  • Sending a quote — when you email, text, generate a PDF, or share a quote, your device hands the content to the app you pick (Mail, Messages, the share sheet, etc.). What happens next is governed by that app's and provider's own privacy policies, not ours.
  • Backups — the export feature creates a backup file that you save or share wherever you choose. You control where that file goes.

Purchases

ResiVolt offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock its quoting features. Payments are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play — we never see or receive your payment card details. To enable and restore your purchase across devices, the app uses RevenueCat, which processes purchase records and app-generated or device identifiers for that purpose. It does not receive the business or customer data you enter into the app.

Children's privacy

ResiVolt is a professional tool intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and the revised policy will be posted at this address.
